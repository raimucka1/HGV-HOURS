What this build assumes

Overtime is per day, not per week. Basic pay covers 9.25 h per contracted day worked; every hour beyond that is paid at 1.5. The 17 Jul slip proves it: a weekly 46.25 h threshold predicts 22.50 OT hours, the daily rule predicts 36.75, and the slip paid 37.25. On the 14 Aug slip both rules agree, which is why the weekly version looked right.
Night premium runs 22:00–08:00, Mon–Fri. Not 06:00. That window reproduces the 14 Aug slip exactly (38.50 h) and lands within 0.25 h on 17 Jul (50.50 calculated vs 50.25 paid). The 06:00 end gives 12.50 and 18.50 — nowhere near. This contradicts the rate card wording, so treat it as a strong hypothesis and re-test on 11 Sep.
Pension is 4% of gross. SMARTT = 4% of total positive payments, exact on both slips. Total Pay is already net of it, so tax and NI both sit on Total Pay.
NI matches to the penny. Cat A, 8% between £966.92 and £3,866.92 per 4-week period, 2% above.
Holiday is not paid at base rate. 17 Jul paid £238.79/day, 14 Aug £232.31/day — a rolling average including premiums, not 9.25 × £19.265 (£178.20). The day rate is a setting; update it when a new slip lands.
Driver Allowance ≈ 0.67% of basic pay. Fits both slips to a penny, but two data points is not a rule. Verify.
PAYE is an estimate. Real PAYE is cumulative across the tax year; this is a non-cumulative calculation on code 1277L. It came out 15p under on 14 Aug and 27p over on 17 Jul.
Two seeded holiday days are a guess. The 17 Jul slip shows 2.00 holiday days. Sun 7 and Sun 21 Jun are the only unexplained gaps, so they are seeded as holiday. Correct them if wrong.
Small log discrepancies. Basic + OT hours come to 203.75 against 203.25 logged for the July period, and 170.25 against 170.50 for August. A shift or two is probably out by a quarter hour.
Pension rate changed. 4% is confirmed on both slips to the penny. The increase to 7.5% is set per period on the Pay tab, so it doesn't retro-break the 17 Jul and 14 Aug checks. If the 11 Sep pension line divided by gross comes out at 4%, the change hadn't landed yet — move it forward a period.
Not modelled: sick pay, bank holidays, the "Pre Tax Pension" positive line (enter it by hand in Settings), and Un-Soc Hrs Prem 20%.
